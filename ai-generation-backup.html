The Problem

Platform histories are convenient until they are the only copy.

Generation tools change quickly. Subscriptions change. Favorites become crowded. Histories get harder to trust. Browser downloads rarely keep the prompt or source context that made the work useful.

A real local backup is not only the file. For AI work, it is the file plus the prompt, creative recipe, source, date, and notes that let you understand it later.

Platforms change

Interface changes, account changes, and shifting histories can make old work harder to reach.

Favorites can disappear into noise

A save button is useful, but a long platform list is not the same as ownership or a durable searchable archive.

Plain downloads are incomplete

The image or video may survive, but prompt history and creative memory often get left behind.
