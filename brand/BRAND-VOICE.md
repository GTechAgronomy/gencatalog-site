_Generated by Okara AI CMO — March 16, 2026_

## 1. Brand Voice & Tone

GenCatalog's copy is direct, empathetic, and quietly confident. It names a real frustration creators feel, then solves it without hype.

**Voice adjectives:** Clear · Practical · Honest · Understated · Creator-first

The tone is conversational but never casual to the point of being sloppy. It respects the reader's intelligence and doesn't oversell. Think: a knowledgeable friend who builds tools, not a startup pitching investors.

---

## 2. Writing Dos and Don'ts

### ✅ Do
- Use "you" — speak directly to the creator ("your best generations," "everything you've ever generated")
- Name the pain before the solution — lead with the problem, earn the pitch
- Be specific — say "model, parameters, dimensions, and source platform" not "all your data"
- Use short, punchy sentences — especially for emphasis ("The image survives. The prompt that made it work disappears.")
- Let features speak plainly — "100% local storage, no account required" beats "privacy-first architecture"
- Use "generations" not "content" — match the language your audience actually uses

### ❌ Don't
- Say "leverage," "synergy," "seamless," "robust," or "powerful solution"
- Use passive voice ("images are saved" → "GenCatalog saves your images")
- Overpromise or use hype words like "revolutionary" or "game-changing"
- Write long paragraphs — break ideas into tight, scannable chunks
- Say "users" — say "creators" or "you"
- Lead with features — lead with the problem or outcome

## 3. Blog Content Guidelines

### Structure
- **Headline** — problem-first or outcome-first
- **Opening hook** — 1–3 sentences naming the frustration
- **Body** — short sections with H2/H3 headers, bullet points where helpful
- **CTA** — one clear call to action at the end

### Length
- Sweet spot: 600–1,000 words for most posts
- Deep tutorials or comparisons: up to 1,500 words
- Never pad — cut anything that doesn't serve the creator

### Headline Style
Lead with the reader's problem or goal:
- ✅ "Why Your Best AI Generations Keep Getting Lost (And How to Fix It)"
- ✅ "How to Build a Searchable AI Image Library Without Cloud Storage"
- ❌ "Introducing GenCatalog's New Filtering Features"

### CTA
- Always at the end of the post
- Keep it simple: "Download GenCatalog free — 7-day trial, no account required."
- Match the CTA to the post topic

## 4. Social Media Voice

### Twitter / X — Punchy & Relatable
Lead with the pain point or a sharp observation. Short sentences. No hashtag spam. One idea per post.

> The prompt disappears. The image stays. That's the problem.

### LinkedIn — Practical & Credible
Slightly longer, outcome-focused. Speak to the workflow problem. Okay to use a short story or before/after framing. Still no jargon.

> Most AI creators have thousands of generations and no way to find the good ones. GenCatalog fixes that — locally, privately, with a library that remains theirs.

### Reddit — Honest & Community-First
Be a participant, not a marketer. Lead with value or a genuine observation. Acknowledge the niche. Never hard-sell.

> Built this because I kept losing my best Higgsfield prompts. Figured others had the same problem.

### Instagram / Visual Platforms — Visual-Led, Caption Supports
Let the screenshot or demo do the work. Caption adds context or a single insight. Under 2 sentences.

## 5. Competitive Positioning

### Three Unoccupied Gaps (as of March 2026)
1. **Browser-based capture** — automatic capture from Grok Imagine, Higgsfield, Digen. No competitor does this.
2. **Privacy-first, no account** — 100% local storage. Cloud competitors cannot replicate this without rebuilding.
3. **Recipe management** — full prompt + model + settings saved as a reusable unit. Nobody has productized this yet.

### Key Differentiator Statement
> No competitor currently matches GenCatalog's combination of automatic browser-side capture + local-first storage + platform-specific metadata preservation across emerging consumer AI video/image platforms.

### Positioning vs. Eagle
Eagle is the main established competitor. Their weakness: manual import only, no prompt/metadata capture, no browser extension for AI platforms. GenCatalog is frictionless by comparison.
